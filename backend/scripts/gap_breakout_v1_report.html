NIFTY Gap Breakout — long and short · gap ≥ 0.3% · cn 09:30–11:00

One rule, two mirrored directions. Step 1 — Gap% = (Open − PDC) / PDC × 100: ≥ +0.3% makes the day a LONG day, ≤ −0.3% a SHORT day, anything between is skipped. Step 2 — the first 15-minute candle (09:15–09:29) sets the level: its high for longs, its low for shorts; the first candle cn between 09:30 and 11:00 that closes beyond that level is the trigger, and the trade is taken at the open of cn+1. Step 3 — stop is cn's low for longs, cn's high for shorts. Step 4 — Risk = |Entry − SL|. Step 5 — Target = Entry + Risk × RR for longs, Entry − Risk × RR for shorts.
A session is either a gap up or a gap down, never both, so the two sides never overlap and Both is simply every signal the rule produces. Squared off at 15:15 if still open. PnL is in NIFTY points and in ₹ for 1 lot (65); shorts are scored as Entry − Exit.
PDC is the previous session's official close (daily candle), not the last 1-minute candle — the 1-minute feed ends at 15:29 and misses the closing print. A close-confirmed stop fills at the candle's close, so the loss is not capped at 1R; the slip column is how far past the stop level the fill landed. When one minute both touches the target and breaks the stop, the stop is taken first.
