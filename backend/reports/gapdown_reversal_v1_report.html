NIFTY Gap-down → 15-minute High-Break Reversal · gap ≥ 0.3% · cn 09:30–11:00

Step 1 — GapDown% = (PDC − TodayOpen) / PDC × 100, traded only when ≥ 0.3%. Step 2 — the first 15-minute candle (09:15–09:29) is the range; the first candle cn between 09:30 and 11:00 whose close is above the range high is the reversal, and the trade is a BUY at the open of cn+1. Step 3 — stop is the low of cn+1 — the entry candle itself — hit when a later candle closes below it, or, in touch mode, the moment price trades at that low. Step 4 — Risk = Entry − SL, Target = Entry + Risk × RR.
cn+1's low only exists once cn+1 has closed, so the stop and the target go live at the open of cn+2; nothing is evaluated inside the entry candle. The “levels live” time is on every row of the day table.
Squared off at 15:15 if still open. The target is a touch on 1-minute data, so a target reached inside a candle is taken before that candle's close can trigger the stop. PnL is in NIFTY points and in ₹ for 1 lot (65).
PDC is the previous session's official close (daily candle), not the last 1-minute candle — the 1-minute feed ends at 15:29 and misses the closing print. A close-confirmed stop fills at the candle's close, so the loss is not capped at 1R; the slip column is how far past the stop level the fill landed. When one minute both touches the target and breaks the stop, the stop is taken first.
